Features & Capabilities

Stay organized, focused, and on top of your academic responsibilities effortlessly. Here is a look at the core features of the Student Task Manager:

  • Clean & Interactive UI: Enjoy a simple, user-friendly interface that makes managing tasks quick and stress-free.
  • Track Assignments: Never miss a deadline by logging all your homework, essays, and major projects with due dates.
  • Prioritize Work: Sort your tasks by High, Medium, or Low priority to tackle the most important goals first.
  • Set Reminders: Keep track of upcoming exams and assignment submission dates through the Notifications page.
  • Smart Alerts: Get notified about overdue tasks, tasks due today, and high-priority pending assignments.
  • Progress Dashboard: View a live summary of completed and pending tasks with a dynamic progress bar.
  • Edit & Complete Tasks: Update task details or mark tasks as completed directly from the Task Manager page.
  • Daily Schedule: Add, edit, and delete your daily class schedule entries with room and teacher details.
  • Data Persistence: All tasks, schedule entries, and profile details are saved using localStorage so nothing is lost on refresh.
